# Legal readiness checklist

The public pages are a transparent beta baseline, not legal advice and not proof of compliance in every jurisdiction. A qualified lawyer must review the actual operator, launch countries, business model, and data flows before commercialization.

## Current product choices

- The public product name is **Reversi Arena**. The UI describes the project as independent and includes a non-affiliation notice for the Othello trademark.
- The beta is 13+, has no wagering or prizes, and does not claim ratings, tournaments, player counts, broadcasts, or features that are not operating.
- The only browser cookie is necessary for a guest session. The current UI has no advertising cookies, behavioral profiling, or third-party analytics.
- Privacy, terms, and fair-play pages describe the data and controls that actually exist, plus clear beta limitations.

## Required before a commercial launch

- Establish and name the legal operator, business address, support and privacy contacts, governing law, dispute process, and launch territories.
- Obtain trademark advice and any required license or approval for product naming, marketing, imagery, tournaments, merchandise, or monetization.
- Produce jurisdiction-specific privacy disclosures, data-processing records, lawful-basis analysis, retention schedule, data-subject request workflow, vendor agreements, international-transfer analysis, and breach procedure.
- Confirm age-assurance and parental-consent obligations for each territory. Do not knowingly process children's data without the required controls.
- Add accessible reporting, moderation, copyright/takedown, appeals, account deletion/export, and law-enforcement request processes.
- Review consumer protection, auto-renewal, refunds, taxes, sanctions, export controls, contest/prize, accessibility, and platform-store requirements before enabling relevant features.
- Document all subprocessors, including Cloudflare, and keep policies synchronized with production behavior.

## Release discipline

Every material change to identity, chat, analytics, advertising, payments, tournaments, prizes, location data, or children’s access requires a privacy/security/legal review before deployment. Policy text must never promise controls that the product does not yet implement.
